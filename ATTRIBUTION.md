## Third-party asset

The rocket body (`art/tenpin_rocket.fbx` and everything in `art/round_textures/`)
is derived from the TRG-122 in:

> "Roketsan Missiles" (https://skfb.ly/pq7qp) by **sakigakefuruzawa**, licensed
> under **Creative Commons Attribution**
> (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

**What was changed:** the TRG-122 was isolated from the set and uniformly scaled
by 0.68644 to a 2.000 m round (from 2.9136 m), giving an 87.4 mm body and a
144.3 mm deployed fin span. Its origin was recentred to the geometric centre for
export. The five 2K PBR maps were unpacked from the source blend unmodified and
renamed from `ITBM 600_Roketsan Missiles_*` to `TenpinRocket_*`.
